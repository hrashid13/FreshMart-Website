Perfect Picnic Pasta Salad

  • rotini pasta
  • cherry tomatoes
  • cucumber
  • feta cheese
  • olive oil
  • lemon juice
  1. Cook pasta according to package directions and cool.
  2. Dice tomatoes and cucumber into small pieces.
  3. Mix pasta, vegetables, and crumbled feta in a large bowl.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice, toss well.
